# Privacy Policy

**Last revised**: 2026-05-06 (draft)

GameNight is a service that helps Game Masters organize tabletop
campaigns. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect
when you use GameNight, how we use it, and the rights you have over it.

---

## What we collect

**Account information.** When you register, we collect your email
address and a securely hashed password. If you set a display name, we
store that too. We do not store passwords in plain text.

**Content you create.** When you use GameNight, you create games,
characters, schedules, availability, and messages. We store this so
that you, your players, and the people you have invited can see it.
You own this content (see our Terms of Service).

**Automatic technical information.** When you use GameNight, our
servers receive standard request information — your IP address, the
type of browser and device you are using, and which pages you
requested. We use this for operational and security purposes and do
not combine it with advertising profiles.

We do **not** collect: precise location data, biometric information,
contact lists, or browsing history outside GameNight.

---

## How we use what we collect

We use the information we collect to:

- Run the service — so that you can sign in, your players can see the
  schedule, and notifications get delivered.
- Communicate with you — about your account, security alerts, and
  service updates.
- Keep the service safe — investigate abuse, fraud, and harmful
  activity.
- Improve the service — fix bugs, understand which features people
  use, plan what to build next.

We do not use your information to build advertising profiles, and we
do not sell your information to anyone.

---

## How we share what we collect

**We do not sell your personal information.**

We share information only with:

- **Service providers** that help us run GameNight — for example,
  hosting providers and email-delivery services. These providers act
  on our behalf and may not use the information for anything else.
- **Law enforcement and others, where required by law** — for
  example, a valid subpoena or a court order. Where we are not legally
  prohibited from telling you, we will.
- **People you have invited** — that is the whole point of inviting
  them. The players in your game see the schedule you publish; the GM
  of a game you join sees your availability.

We do not share your content with anyone outside the people you have
invited and our service providers.

---

## Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies for one purpose: keeping you signed in. The cookie is
marked `HttpOnly`, `Secure`, and `SameSite=Lax` so it is not exposed
to JavaScript, only sent over HTTPS, and not sent in cross-site
requests. We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or
third-party analytics that profile users.

If your browser blocks the auth cookie, GameNight cannot keep you
signed in.

---

## Your rights

You have rights over your personal information. Depending on where
you live, the law may give you the rights to:

- **Access** the information we hold about you.
- **Correct** inaccurate information.
- **Delete** your information.
- **Export** a copy of your information in a portable format.
- **Object** to certain uses, or **restrict** processing.

You can exercise these rights at any time by emailing
**{contactEmail}**. We will reply within a reasonable time and will
not charge you for ordinary requests.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or
Switzerland, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your
local data-protection authority. If you are a California resident,
the California Consumer Privacy Act and California Privacy Rights Act
give you additional rights, including the right to know what personal
information we have collected and the right to delete it; the
mechanisms above honor those rights.

---

## How long we keep your information

We keep your account information for as long as your account is open.
If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account
information within thirty days, except where we are legally required
to keep records longer (for example, to respond to a tax audit).

We may keep aggregated, de-identified information indefinitely for
operational and analytical purposes.

---

## Children

GameNight is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly
collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a
child has created an account, email **{contactEmail}** and we will
remove the account.

---

## International transfers

GameNight is operated from the United States. If you use GameNight
from outside the United States, you understand that your information
will be transferred to and processed in the United States. We will
publish detail about transfer safeguards in the final Privacy Policy.

---

## How we keep your information safe

We use industry-standard safeguards:

- All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
- Passwords are hashed with a modern, slow algorithm.
- Access to production data is restricted to a small number of
  staff and is logged.
- We will notify affected users of any security incident that
  materially affects their information, in accordance with applicable
  law.

No service can promise perfect security. If you suspect your account
has been compromised, email **{contactEmail}** immediately.

---

## Changes to this policy

We will revise this policy. When we do, we will update the date at
the top of this page. For material changes, we will notify
signed-in users by email at least seven days before the change takes
effect.

---

Questions about this policy? Email **{contactEmail}**. You can also
